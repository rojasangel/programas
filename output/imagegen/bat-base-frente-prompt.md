Show its flat round end face, gently rounded rim and short handle continuing down and away toward the lower right. No extra ornament, no invented number sticker or size printed on the face, no other brand names. A glimpse of the original white FR branding on the distant shaft is acceptable only if natural to the framing; do not add a new brand to the knob.
Scene/backdrop: soft natural daylight, out-of-focus green baseball-field background similar to the example, clean professional e-commerce photography. Portrait composition, shallow depth of field, realistic reflections in black varnish and natural wood grain; no hands or people.
Constraints: one single bat, not a collage; no UI, border, caption, watermark, annotation, decorative chrome ring, Rawlings mark, red badge, or unsupported product feature. The view must clearly look INTO the end of the knob, not show the whole bat from the side.
